Applied AI & evaluation · Feb – Apr 2026

EduLLM & evaluator improvements

A multi-agent exam-question generator and evidence-grounded improvements to its shared evaluation system.

Shipped internally

My role

Built the generator from scratch and contributed changes adopted by the shared evaluator team.

The problem

Generating convincing questions is easy compared with generating correct ones—and a confidently wrong evaluator can hide the failure.

What I built

  • Parallel subject-expert generation followed by synthesis and quality evaluation.
  • Grounded fact checking, structured question formats and generation across multiple grades.
  • Deterministic scoring and fact-audit improvements for the shared evaluator.
  • Adaptive concurrency and deployment of the generation service.

Decisions that mattered

  • Investigated suspiciously high scores using repeated runs and manually checked examples.
  • Separated factual correctness from stylistic quality and schema validity.
  • Brought concrete evidence and proposed fixes to the evaluator team instead of arguing from a leaderboard.

Where it landed

Shipped the generator, improved evaluation reliability and was named as an inventor on a filed patent application for the concurrency design. This is an application, not a granted patent.
