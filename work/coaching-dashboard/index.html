Coaching Dashboard

Operational workspaces for coaching teams: scheduling, session context, notes, analytics, insights and escalations.

Production product

My role

Sole engineer across the core product, data migration, integrations and production delivery.

The problem

Different roles needed a consistent view of sessions spread across scheduling systems, tracker data, recordings and AI analysis—without broadening access to private records.

What I built

  • Coach, guide and admin workspaces with scoped navigation and actions.
  • Unified session ingestion, duplicate reconciliation, recording discovery and AI-analysis orchestration.
  • Pre-call context, post-call notes, insight emails, escalation workflows and demand visualization.
  • Scoped MCP tools and an analytics surface backed by the same business services as the application.
  • A DynamoDB-to-Postgres migration with integration coverage and explicit service/data-access boundaries.

Decisions that mattered

  • Kept authorization and transaction boundaries in business services rather than page components.
  • Used durable background delivery for work that must survive a transient external failure.
  • Checked analytics against source records, not just whether a chart rendered.
  • Built prototypes and discussed workflows with the people who would use the product.

Where it landed

Turned a recording-analysis tool into a broader operations platform, with self-service workflows and a documented handover.
