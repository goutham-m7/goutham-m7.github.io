Realtime AI & desktop · Apr – Nov 2026

Alphi

A realtime voice-coaching experience combining speech, on-screen context and a native desktop application.

Shipped with real users

My role

Worked on the first version and led the later rebuild, including the coaching loop and evaluation infrastructure.

The problem

A helpful voice coach has to understand the moment, let the learner think, handle interruptions and respond without simply giving the answer away.

What I built

  • A Rust/Tauri desktop client with microphone and screen context, audio playback and signed distribution.
  • A WebSocket relay connecting realtime voice providers to the desktop experience.
  • Turn-taking, echo and interruption handling, screen perception and answer-validation logic.
  • Replay evaluation, simulated learner scenarios and calibrated model-judge checks.
  • Local and headless verification paths, controlled experiments and release automation.

Decisions that mattered

  • Separated speech recognition, perception, turn ownership and coaching quality instead of treating them as one score.
  • Used replay experiments to compare changes while keeping their limits distinct from live outcomes.
  • Chose model and prompt changes based on measured behavior, not cost or novelty alone.
  • Kept learning-data privacy and release verification in the development process.

Where it landed

Shipped desktop releases and went live with students. Later improvements were assessed through replay experiments; current usage is not claimed.

Toolkit

  • TypeScript
  • WebSockets
  • Realtime voice APIs
  • Speech / vision
  • Evaluation
