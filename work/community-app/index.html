Full-stack collaboration · Jan – Feb 2026

Community app & CRM integration

Community, family and onboarding features, with a durable pipeline connecting application data to HubSpot.

Production contribution

My role

Contributed within a wider engineering team; owned the notification system and initial CRM-sync implementation.

The problem

User-facing changes needed to reach an external CRM reliably without making onboarding depend on a slow or unavailable third-party API.

What I built

  • Domain-event notifications and a realtime notification bell.
  • Referral and onboarding flows, family features, connection summaries, chat UI and presence contributions.
  • One-way app-to-HubSpot synchronization using transactional enqueue, pgmq and Deno workers.
  • Idempotent entity handling, retries, dead-letter alerts and onboarding prefill.
  • Circle-scoped visibility and row-level privacy controls.

Decisions that mattered

  • Committed application changes and queue messages together.
  • Made processors resolve their own dependencies instead of requiring perfect event order.
  • Kept CRM identifiers out of client-facing surfaces.
  • Kept the scope honest: the later bidirectional CRM rewrite and messaging-layer design were not my work.

Where it landed

Contributed to a live family/community application and shipped its notification and CRM-sync workflows with the team.

Toolkit

  • Supabase / Postgres
  • pgmq
  • Deno
  • HubSpot
