Scheduling & infrastructure · Jun – Oct 2026

OpenCal

A scheduling platform spanning availability, routing, booking, calendar synchronization and partner integrations.

Production product

My role

Sole engineer and end-to-end product owner, using AI coding agents for implementation under my design and verification.

The problem

Booking a free time is only part of scheduling. Eligibility, buffers, capacity, fairness, calendar state and partner expectations all have to agree.

What I built

  • Availability and routing engines, temporary holds, confirmation, cancellation, rescheduling and coach transfers.
  • A multi-tenant admin console with event types, routing forms, attributes, working hours and workflow configuration.
  • Partner booking APIs, signed webhooks, single sign-on integration and calendar handoff.
  • A rebuilt backend with PostgreSQL, background workers, a transactional outbox and AWS infrastructure as code.

Decisions that mattered

  • Protected booking invariants in the database, not just through application pre-checks.
  • Used replay and live-database scenarios to distinguish a more densely packed schedule from an increase in actual demand.
  • Profiled latency before changing the design; removed repeated queries and formatting work.
  • Treated migration, partner compatibility, monitoring and operational handover as part of the product—not post-launch cleanup.

Where it landed

Went from a customer demo to a production scheduling replacement, then through a second-generation rebuild and controlled cutover.
